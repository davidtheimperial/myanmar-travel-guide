About this guide

The Myanmar Travel Guide is an independent, student-made educational project about the country's culture, history and landmarks. It exists to explain Myanmar clearly and to point readers towards sources that can give current, authoritative advice.

By David Gaius · Updated 26 November 2026

Who writes it

Myanmar Travel Guide is written and maintained by David Gaius, an independent writer with an interest in Southeast Asian history and culture. It is a personal, non-commercial project rather than the work of a newsroom, a tour operator or a government body, and it does not carry advertising or sell anything.

The writing aims to be plain, specific and honest about what is known and what is contested. Where the country's politics are disputed — and much of Myanmar's recent history is — the guide says so rather than presenting one version as settled fact.

How it is researched and sourced

Each section draws on public reference material and the reporting of established organisations, and links to its sources so you can check them yourself. The main references are:

  • Wikipedia for general history and culture, cross-checked against other sources;
  • UN OCHA and UNICEF for humanitarian and displacement figures;
  • the UK FCDO and the US State Department for travel advice;
  • the ICRC for humanitarian context; and
  • UNESCO for heritage listings such as Bagan.

Figures that change often, such as displacement and humanitarian need, are dated on the page and should be read as a snapshot. The travel advisory is reviewed whenever those figures are updated.

What this site is not

This is not a travel agency, a booking service or a source of safety advice. It cannot verify conditions on the ground, and nothing here should be read as an endorsement of travel to Myanmar at any particular time. For anything time-sensitive, use the official sources above and your own government's guidance. The travel advisory explains this in full.

Photography and media

All photographs and the one video clip come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, with the photographers credited. The ambient music is synthesised in your browser and is not a recorded file. Full attributions are on the image credits page.

Corrections and contact

If you spot something out of date or wrong, please raise it on GitHub. Corrections, especially to time-sensitive facts, are welcome.

How to cite this guide

Gaius, David. Myanmar Travel Guide. https://myanmartravelguide.netlify.app/ (accessed 26 November 2026). Please cite the specific page you used and check the figures against the primary sources it links to.
