Practical

Before you go

When to go

November to February is the cool, dry season and the easiest time to travel. March to May is hot; June to October brings the monsoon.

Check safety first

Foreign-office advice changes often, and some regions are closed to visitors entirely. Check your own government's advisory and your embassy before booking anything.

Etiquette

Shoes and socks come off at pagodas. Cover shoulders and knees. Ask before photographing people or monks, and never point your feet at a Buddha image.

A note on this guide
